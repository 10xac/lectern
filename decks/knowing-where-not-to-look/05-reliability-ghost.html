Per-source reliability and ghost mass

For each source s, triage predicts reliability r: the chance it answers correctly if the answer is in its corpus, and ghost mass g: the chance the answer is not in its corpus at all.

More sampling cannot reduce ghost mass. Only another source can.

lectern fuses the sources two ways: a Bayesian posterior with its evidence Z, and Dempster-Shafer with its conflict kappa, shown side by side.

The joint ghost mass gamma is at most the smallest per-source g, and equals their product only if the sources fail independently.
