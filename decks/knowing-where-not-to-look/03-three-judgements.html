Three judgements before any search

  • The hurdle: out of scope, ill posed or caught by guardrails. Decline or reframe first.
  • The rare passage: the answer is held somewhere the agent seldom goes. Look there on purpose.
  • The ghost passage: the answer is written nowhere. Stop looking; derive, ask or abstain in the open.
The ghost is a property of the question and its answer together: questions whose answers are unwritten are rarely asked either.
