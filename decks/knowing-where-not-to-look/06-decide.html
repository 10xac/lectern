Answer, qualify, ask or abstain

  • Answer when the fused probability of the best answer is above one half.
  • Qualify when it is close to one half, or when the sources clash.
  • Ask a clarifying question when the question itself is ambiguous.
  • Abstain when no source holds the answer, and say which sources were checked.
Close: the agent that knows where not to look spends its effort where an answer can exist.
