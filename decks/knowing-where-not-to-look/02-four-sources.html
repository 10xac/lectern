Four sources, four failure modes

SourceWhat it isHow it fails
Pthe model's own weightsfluent and stale; silent about what it never saw
Wthe open web and the user's own turnsfresh and contradictory
Ka trusted, curated knowledge basereliable but narrow
Mthe system's memory of past answersspecific, but can entrench its own mistakes
A page moves from W to K by curation, not by being retrieved often. M is kept apart from K because the system writes it itself.
